Master's Thesis: User Study Data Analysis

End-to-end Python analysis of a controlled 24-participant study comparing two XR training designs, from raw-data integration and validation to statistical testing and reporting.

What I did

  • Merged four raw datasets, validated the data, and reshaped it from wide to long format in a reproducible pandas workflow.
  • Compared task time, usability (SUS), workload (NASA-TLX) and discomfort (SSQ) between the 2D and 3D conditions.
  • Checked assumptions with Shapiro–Wilk (normality) and Levene's test (equal variance), then chose an independent t-test or a Mann–Whitney U test for each measure.
  • Found an extreme usability outlier (SUS 2.5/100) and reported results both with and without it, instead of simply removing it.

Findings

  • 3D ghost hands showed slight advantages in task completion time (median 117s vs 138s), workload (NASA-TLX 3.33 vs 3.50) and physical comfort (simulator sickness score 15 vs 30, lower is better).
  • Perceived usability was slightly higher for 2D labels (SUS 87.5 vs 85.0), and both designs were well above the usability benchmark of 68.
  • None of the differences were statistically significant; the results were published in three peer-reviewed papers.

CRM Sales Pipeline Analysis

8,800 B2B sales opportunities from five related tables (pipeline, accounts, products, teams), cleaned, validated and merged into one master dataset.

Questions

  • How does each sales team perform, and are any agents lagging behind?
  • Are there quarter-over-quarter trends, and do some products win more often?

Findings

  • West leads in revenue ($3.57M), but East has the highest win rate (51.1%).
  • Low-revenue agents still win 60%+ of deals, so the issue is pipeline volume, not closing skill.
  • Revenue grew from $524K to $2.97M in two quarters while win rate fell: a shift toward higher-value deals.
  • GTK 500 is the only underperforming product (37.5% win rate vs 46–50%).

HR Employee Attrition

Who leaves, where and why, across 1,470 employees. Coded fields mapped to labels in Power Query; age, tenure and income groups built with DAX.

Findings

  • Overtime is the strongest driver: 30.5% attrition vs 10.4% without overtime.
  • New employees (0–1 year) leave at 34.9%, and Sales Representatives at 39.8%.
  • Low income employees leave at 28.6% vs 5.6% for very high income.

Predicting Student Pass/Fail

Exploratory analysis and classification of 349 students from the UCI Student Performance dataset, using only demographic, family and behavioural features.

What I did

  • Excluded earlier-period grades on purpose to avoid data leakage, and used a stratified 80/20 train/test split.
  • Compared Logistic Regression and Random Forest with precision, recall, F1 and confusion matrices (both about 66% accuracy).
  • Found absences, past failures and health to be the most important features.
  • Saved the model with pickle and used it to predict a new, unseen student.
